## Exact prompt

Create a production game texture atlas for a 1990s gothic punk nocturnal city. Reference image is the APPROVED existing facade atlas; match its quiet near-black masonry, crisp restrained trim, sparse small amber interiors. This is a NEW companion atlas, not a scene or concept. Square raster, exactly THREE full-width horizontal facade strips, each occupying exactly one third height, no gutters, no margins, no labels. Each strip is exactly TWO floors: upper floor top half, ground floor bottom half. Perfect flat orthographic front elevation, no perspective, roof, ground, cast shadow or sky. All windows narrow/tall, widely spaced, black mass dominant, low contrast surface texture, no speckled wear or ornate busy pattern. One entrance exactly in center of ground floor per strip, quiet bays on both sides useful for modular repetition. Row1: severe elegant 1930s ART DECO civic facade, charcoal cut stone large blocks, restrained fluted piers, central bronze double door, narrow tall rectangular black windows and one small amber window at x .22 of row. Row2: old university collegiate gothic academic facade, smooth soot-black limestone, restrained lancet windows with simple tracery, dignified centered double oak door with warm transom, one lit upper window at x .78. Row3: decadent luxury hotel/passage facade, black stone, thin tarnished bronze frames and subtle green-black glazed bays, central glazed bronze vestibule with small warm light and two flank dark shopfronts, one amber upper window at x .22. No text, signs, crosses, banners, people, lamps protruding, balconies or ornament extending outside strip. Sharp edge readability at game size. Whole sheet remains dark, light comes only from a FEW localized windows. Maintain floor alignment and coherent geometry.
