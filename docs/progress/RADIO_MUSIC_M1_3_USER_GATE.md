## What not to judge yet

General MIDI timbres are placeholders. Do not reject a station because its current piano, bass, drum kit, strings or FX sound cheap. The user owns final instrument/sample replacement, mix and mastering in the DAW.

Judge:

- groove;
- source recognisability;
- 1990s genre credibility;
- station distinctness;
- fit with ViceBlood while driving at night.

## Attribution gate

All four proofs use Public Domain score/manuscript sources documented in the seed manifests. No commercial recording, famous break recording, stem or third-party sample is a dependency. Every proof has a ready-to-use composer/work player credit.
