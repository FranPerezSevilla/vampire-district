# Urban material pass
Goal: replace schematic facade dashes and clean pavement with coherent architectural detail.
Authority: BuildingParallax facade projection and CitySurfacePresentationPolicy; visual changes only.
Scope: projected recessed windows, selected warm lit panes, masonry frames, shutters, entrances, cornices, drainpipes and pasted bills; deterministic pavement stains, cracks and litter flecks.
Non-goals: collisions, generated geometry, traffic, new animation loops.
Acceptance: every facade panel remains on its projected plane, detail stays within visible surface bounds, stationary camera caching remains intact, browser boot and occlusion remain functional.
