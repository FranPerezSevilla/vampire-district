# City identity and infill
Goal: recognisable cathedral/police landmarks and denser, varied neighbourhoods.
Authority: BuildingIdentity and the existing building palette/parallax renderer; compiler infill pass.
Scope: overhead copper cruciform cathedral roof and twin bell towers, police shield/communications complex, six restrained material families, eighteen small buildings distributed across districts, at most three per district.
Non-goals: changing road layout, power rules, camera or existing roof routes.
Acceptance: roofs cover their collision footprint, landmark identity is readable, infill avoids roads/landmark sites/access points and existing solids. Validate city and regenerate streaming artifacts.
