# Milestone 15.7 — Blood Sense, contextual Whisper and the Beast

_Last updated: 2026-07-29_

**Status: ✅ Delivered in PR #46.**

## Goal

Turn the existing three prototype powers into one coherent predator toolkit:

- Blood Sense reads living bodies, wounds, feeding traces and learned political marks rather than exposing arbitrary game data;
- Whisper offers small commands selected from the current target and context rather than one automatic lure;
- high Hunger changes temptation and control through readable modifiers;
- the player may voluntarily **Give In** for short power at a concrete evidence cost;
- no system reveals emotions, personalities or emotion-derived bonuses.

## Blood Sense

Blood Sense remains a short, direct pulse rather than a permanent wallhack.

It reads:

- living heartbeats through thin cover and outside the immediate camera;
- wounded or downed prey;
- recently bitten and fully fed victims;
- drained bodies and fresh blood trails;
- supernatural beings marked with no heartbeat as silence/absence;
- faction-protected victims only when that protection is already known to the player.

High Hunger increases heartbeat intensity and effective predator-reading range. It does not reveal hidden political information by itself.

## Contextual Whisper

Pressing the Whisper input on a valid nearby mind opens the existing interaction-choice UI. The commands offered depend on target state and local context:

- **Come here** — follow the player briefly;
- **Walk away** — leave the player and current scene;
- **Stay calm** — suppress immediate panic and a pending report when the mind is still reachable;
- **Forget this** — remove only latent witness memory; it cannot erase a completed report or institutional knowledge;
- **Open it** — request an explicit nearby `whisperOpen` context exposed by a site, mission or prop;
- **Get in** — enter a nearby stopped non-police vehicle as a temporary passenger;
- **Call them off** — available only to a suitable authority target and reduces local Heat without deleting Exposure evidence.

Each command has:

- a Hunger cost;
- range and target rules;
- deterministic resistance;
- visible feedback;
- witness/evidence consequences when the command is observed or resisted.

Hunters are immune to ordinary Whisper. Alerted, trained or hostile targets resist more commands; resistance is never a hidden random roll.

## The Beast

Hunger has four readable pressure states:

```text
CONTROLLED
STRAINED
RAVENOUS
CRITICAL
```

Higher Hunger:

- makes heartbeats more prominent;
- slightly accelerates direct feeding;
- reduces the subtlety of Whisper;
- does not seize input or force an arbitrary action.

The new remappable **Give In** input activates a short voluntary burst:

- faster movement;
- faster feeding;
- stronger melee impact;
- immediate supernatural recovery from hit stun where valid;
- added Hunger;
- visible-power evidence when witnessed;
- a cooldown that prevents permanent uptime.

## Runtime ownership

- pure predator rules live in `phaser/src/data/predator-powers.js`;
- `PowersSystem` owns Blood Sense, Whisper commands and Give In runtime state;
- `NpcSystem` executes temporary Whisper movement/passenger intents;
- `FeedingSystem`, movement and melee consume Beast multipliers;
- `WitnessSystem` and `ExposureSystem` remain the knowledge/evidence authorities;
- the input system owns the remappable `beast` action.

## Deliberate limits

- no emotion, mood or blood-resonance system;
- no dialogue tree generated by Whisper;
- no permanent passive x-ray mode;
- no involuntary frenzy or random control seizure;
- no broad passenger, interior-door or faction-authority simulation beyond explicit contextual hooks;
- no vampire population expansion in this slice.

## Acceptance

- Blood Sense distinguishes living, wounded, fed, drained and heartbeat-absent subjects;
- protected marks appear only after player knowledge exists;
- Whisper opens contextual options and never offers impossible commands;
- Forget This cannot erase already reported/institutional evidence;
- resistant targets fail deterministically and create readable consequences;
- high Hunger changes predator modifiers without taking control;
- Give In has useful movement/feeding/melee effects and concrete evidence risk;
- input bindings, UI feedback and diagnostics expose the new state;
- 322 unit tests cover the pure/runtime additions;
- city validation and both city compilers remain green;
- boot, campaign and systems Chromium suites provide the final integration gate.
