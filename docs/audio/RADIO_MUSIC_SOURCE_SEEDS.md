# M1.2 radio music source seeds

Checked: **2026-08-22**.

This file records the four public-domain source works selected for the first station proof cycle. These are score/source references only. No third-party recording, performance, stem or commercial sample is part of the canonical M1.3 input.

The paired candidate JSON files under `phaser/assets/audio/radio-midi/<station-id>/` are the machine-readable authority for credit/provenance fields.

## Blood City Beats — Chopin

- Candidate ID: `chopin-prelude-04-boombap-a`
- Work: **Prelude in E minor, Op. 28 No. 4** — Frédéric Chopin (1838–39)
- Source: Wessel & Co., London, ca.1839, *24 Preludes Op.28*, Book I (Nos.1–14), plate W. & Co. No. 3098
- Source catalogue: IMSLP `#66277`
- Source page: `https://imslp.org/wiki/Preludes,_Op.28_(Chopin,_Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric)`
- Status relied on: IMSLP lists this score as **Public Domain**.
- Planned proof lane: dark 1990s boom bap / trip-hop, ~88 BPM.
- Player credit: `“Prelude in E minor, Op. 28 No. 4” — Frédéric Chopin (1838–39). Arranged for ViceBlood (2026).`

Reason for selection: immediately usable descending harmonic identity, very little source material is needed for recognition, and it avoids reusing the main-theme Gnossienne in the first radio proof set.

## Night Shift — Grieg

- Candidate ID: `mountain-king-bigbeat-a`

- Composition: 1874–75; selected suite/piano source prepared in 1888
- Source: composer's holograph piano manuscript dated 21 January 1888, Bergen Public Library (`N-Bo`)
- Source catalogue: IMSLP `#810457`
- Source page: `https://imslp.org/wiki/Peer_Gynt_Suite_No.1,_Op.46_(Grieg,_Edvard)`
- Status relied on: IMSLP lists the holograph manuscript as **Public Domain**.
- Planned proof lane: late-1990s big beat / breakbeat / industrial dance, ~138 BPM.
- Player credit: `“In the Hall of the Mountain King” — Edvard Grieg (1874–75). Arranged for ViceBlood (2026).`

Reason for selection: the repeating rising motif naturally supports escalating breakbeat structure without requiring a borrowed drum break or commercial recording.

## M1.3 safety boundary

For all four proofs:

- transcribe only the musical material needed from the source score;
- do not use or imitate a specific modern recording/arrangement;
- drums/bass/secondary parts are original ViceBlood arrangement work;
- no famous break sample recordings;
- source-derived tracks remain named and separate from original arrangement tracks;
- update each JSON manifest with actual duration, track list and SHA-256 when its MIDI is generated.
